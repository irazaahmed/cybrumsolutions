Open the Instagram page of almost any boutique, salon, bakery, or online store in Pakistan and the story is the same: the feed is the catalogue, and the DMs are the checkout counter. "Price plz", "available in size M?", "kab tak deliver hoga" — this is where the actual buying conversation happens, long before anyone visits a website. The problem is that a DM inbox does not scale the way a website does. It closes when you sleep, it gets buried under comments and story replies, and every unanswered message is a customer who found someone else who replied first.

That is the gap Instagram automation on CS Chatbot closes: the same AI assistant that already answers on your website or your WhatsApp number now answers in your Instagram DMs too, without you touching a keyboard.

## Why this matters more here than almost anywhere else

Instagram is not a secondary channel for a lot of Pakistani businesses — for many, it is the storefront. No website, no app, sometimes not even a proper price list, just a feed and an inbox. That works fine at ten messages a day. It stops working the moment a post gets shared around and the DMs go from ten a day to two hundred, and it is exactly the businesses growing the fastest who feel this pain first.

## What connecting Instagram actually does

You connect your own Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account from your CS Chatbot dashboard — a normal login and approval screen, nothing technical on your end. From that point on, every DM your account receives goes through the same pipeline your website widget already uses: the assistant reads your real business knowledge (your site, your uploaded price list or catalogue PDF, your policies), answers grounded in what it actually knows, and cites where the answer came from.

Nothing about your account changes for the people messaging you. They are still talking to your Instagram page, in the same DM thread they always used. The difference is that now someone is always the one replying, and that someone actually knows your business.

## One knowledge base, one Leads tab, three channels

This is the part that matters most for how you run the business day to day: Instagram is not a separate bot with its own setup. It is the same knowledge base as your website and your WhatsApp Business number, and every conversation — whichever channel it started on — lands in the same Leads tab in your dashboard, tagged by channel. Update your price list once and all three channels answer with the new numbers. Get asked something the bot cannot answer, and that gap shows up in the same "Unanswered" list regardless of whether the question came in on a comment reply, a DM, or your site.

If someone messages "kab tak deliver hoga" on Instagram and asks the exact same thing on WhatsApp a week later, they get a consistent, correct answer both times — not two different guesses from two different tools.

## It still knows when to say "I don't know"

A DM assistant that confidently makes up an answer about your stock or your price is worse than no assistant at all — it burns the trust the whole conversation depended on. CS Chatbot only answers from what it actually knows about your business. When a question falls outside that, it says so honestly and hands the conversation to you, instead of guessing and hoping the customer does not notice.
